## 2. The day-change rules (FROZEN; `packages/engine/src/dayChange.ts`)

### 2.1 Terms
- **Held holding:** an instrument of kind `stock`, `etf`, `managed_fund` or `crypto` with engine `openUnits > 0`; or **a bullion holding** (D148): one per metal, made of the Other Assets rows with `price_source = 'bullion'` and engine `remainingUnits > 0`, grouped by the metal the web prices them with (`metal ?? 'silver'`, as `assets/inputs.ts` `otherAssetPricing`). **Cash is never an input** (it is not an instrument); hand-priced other assets (collectibles and the rest) are never inputs (D148).
- **P:** the holding's effective AUD price, exactly the engine's (`PriceItem.price`: manual wins, else the last good fetched price). **valueCents** is the engine's. **Bullion:** P is the metal's spot in AUD per troy ounce (`XAU_AUD_OZ`/`XAG_AUD_OZ`, the value the web used); the holding's units are ounces (`Σ remainingUnits × ozPerUnit` of its rows); its `valueCents` is `Σ` of its rows' engine `valueCents` (each row rounded as on the Other Assets page, so the phone and the web agree to the cent).
- **Session:** the trading session the stored day belongs to (`day_quotes.session_date` in `time_zone`). Listed holdings: Yahoo's latest regular session (the bars' date in the exchange's zone). Managed funds: the date of the last NAV (the last finite daily close, §5.1). Crypto: the Melbourne calendar day (the server's zone), from 00:00 (D142). **Bullion (D153):** the Melbourne calendar day (the server's zone), from 00:00, like crypto.
- **Dates of instants** (a point, a price's as-of, `generatedAt`) are always taken **in an explicit IANA zone with `Intl.DateTimeFormat`** (as `localDateInZone` in `providers/exchangeTime.ts`): the row's `timeZone` for session questions, `DayChangeInput.timeZone` (the server's zone) for "today". **Never the process TZ** (`new Date().getDate()`, `localIsoDate`) inside the engine.
- **B (previous close, AUD):** `previousCloseNative × fxPrev`. AUD instruments: `fxPrev = 1`. Others: `fxPrev` is `convertToAud('1', nativeCurrency, prevRates).fxRate` with `prevRates` built from `market_quotes.previous_close` (`AUDUSD` for USD; `FX_<CCY>AUD` for others; `GBp`/`GBX` as today ÷ 100). `fxPrev` is **null** (→ `no_base`) when the FX series' `previous_close_date` is more than 7 calendar days before the holding's session date (a stuck FX feed). Crypto: B is the stored 00:00 price (CoinGecko is quoted in AUD). **Bullion (D153):** B (AUD per ounce) is the stored **AUD spot at 00:00 Melbourne** (futures USD/oz ÷ `AUDUSD`, both at their last bar at or before midnight; §5.4a); the row is in AUD, so `fxPrev = 1`, and the currency move since midnight is inside the spot (D143 holds exactly; §2.1's FX approximation does not apply).
- **Accepted approximation (FX):** `fxPrev` is the FX series' own previous close (a London-midnight day), not the FX rate at the listing's previous session close. On a Monday morning, a US holding still showing Friday's NY session combines Friday's stock move with the FX move since the last London close. D143 ("including the currency move") is met to within that difference (§15 item 23).
- **Lot classes** (per holding, against its session date, both plain dates; `tradeDate` has no zone and is read as a date in the session's zone):
  - `tradeDate < sessionDate` → **old** (in `oldUnits`);
  - `tradeDate = sessionDate` → **new**: measured from the lot's price;
  - `tradeDate > sessionDate` → **after the session** (including future-dated trades: the engine does not filter trades by date): the units were not held during the stored session, so they add **0 to `dayCents` and 0 to `baseCents`** (they still count in `valueCents`, weight and units).
  - `newUnits = Σ remaining` of new lots; `laterUnits = Σ remaining` of after-session lots; `oldUnits = openUnits − newUnits − laterUnits` (never negative).
  - **Accepted approximation (foreign sessions):** a trade date is the owner's Melbourne date, so a lot bought in a US session is usually dated the next Melbourne day: in that session it counts as after-session (0), and in the next session as new (from its purchase price). M16 pins this. ASX and crypto sessions share the server's date, so they are exact.
  - **Bullion rows as lots:** each held bullion row is one lot of `remainingUnits × ozPerUnit` ounces with `tradeDate = purchaseDate` and an AUD price per ounce of `unitCost × purchaseFxRate ÷ ozPerUnit` (1 for an AUD row's FX). A row **without a purchase date is old**; a **new** row whose cost or purchase FX is unknown is measured from B (as old). The session is the Melbourne day, so the classes are exact, as for crypto (a purchase dated today is new, M22; a future-dated one is after the session, M23). Sales recorded on the Other Assets page have already reduced `remainingUnits` (sold ounces count nothing, as §2.2).

### 2.2 Per holding (`dayStatus`, in this order)
1. Price null (unpriced) → `unpriced`: no day figures; value null; listed last.
2. `priceStatus = 'manual'` (a hand price) → `manual`: no day figures (there is no previous hand price); value and weight count.
3. `priceStatus` `stale` or `failed` → `stale`: no day figures (a stale price's last session is not "today"); value and weight count. **Exception, daily-priced funds (D150):** a holding of kind `managed_fund` whose `priceStatus` is `stale` (not `failed`) and whose day row is `1d` is **not** stopped here when its row's `sessionDate` is no earlier than the `FUND_DAY_MAX_WEEKDAYS`-th weekday before `localDate` (Monday → the previous Thursday; Tuesday → the previous Friday); it goes on to rule 4. Its `priceStatus` stays `stale` (the web is unchanged); the app labels it "DAILY PRICE · <ddd dd/mm>".
   - **A bullion holding's `priceStatus`** comes from its rows' engine `priceStatus`: `fresh` when every held row is `fresh` (priced from the spot), else `stale` when any row has a price (the stored fallback, or a spot the market rule calls stale), else `none` (rule 1). Rows of one metal share one spot, so in practice they agree; a row with no metal (the schema allows it; the importer never writes one) is priced from its fallback and makes the silver holding `stale`. Bullion is never `manual` (hand-priced other assets are not inputs).
4. `no_base` (no day figures; value and weight count; the native % is still given when its own previous close is known) when any of:
   - there is no day row, or B is unknown or ≤ 0;
   - **the price is not from the row's session:** the date of the fetched price's `asOf` in the row's `timeZone` ≠ `row.sessionDate`, or the fetched price's currency ≠ `row.nativeCurrency` (a price from a newer session than the stored bars, e.g. the 5d fallback, or a first post-open fetch that carried no regular bars, would otherwise be measured against an older session's close);
   - (crypto) `row.sessionDate` ≠ `localDate` (no chart since 00:00).
   - (bullion) as crypto: `row.sessionDate` ≠ `localDate` (no bullion row written since 00:00). The spot's as-of is **not** compared with the session date: when the futures have not traded since midnight (Sunday, a Monday before the reopen, a Saturday after the close, a CME holiday) the day's base **is** the spot (§5.4a), so the holding is `ok` with a day of **0** (flat since midnight; §2.4).
5. Otherwise `ok`:
   - `day = oldUnits × (P − B) + Σ newLots remaining × (P − lot price)` (after-session lots add nothing); **`dayCents = centsOf(day)`** (one rounding per holding, half away from zero, never −0).
   - `changePerUnit = P − B`; `dayRatio = (P − B) ÷ B` (the AUD price move, independent of units and lots).
   - Non-AUD: `native = { currency, price: nativePrice, previousClose: previousCloseNative, dayRatio: (nativePrice − previousCloseNative) ÷ previousCloseNative }` (D143's "% in its own currency").
   - **Bullion:** the same formula in ounces: `day = oldOz × (P − B) + Σ newRows oz × (P − row price per oz)`; `changePerUnit` and `dayRatio` per ounce, in AUD; `native = { currency: 'USD', price: the futures' USD/oz now, previousClose: the futures' USD/oz at 00:00 Melbourne (its own `series_day_quotes` row), dayRatio }` (the detail's "Day % in USD", D143), null when that row or its base is missing.
- Sold units are gone: a sell in the session (FIFO, from the oldest lots) leaves fewer old units and no day figure for what was sold. A holding sold out in the session is not held and is not listed.
- `weightRatio = valueCents ÷ totals.valueCents` across the four kinds and bullion (null when unpriced or the total is 0). Ratios are decimal strings at **12 significant digits**, half up, normalised (`ratioString` in `packages/engine/src/num.ts`; the Kotlin parser and the fixtures depend on this exact form).

### 2.3 Totals
- `valueCents = Σ valueCents` of held, priced holdings (equal to Σ of the four kinds' `summary.valueCents` **plus Σ the Other Assets page's bullion rows' `valueCents`**: the acceptance test).
- `dayCents = Σ dayCents` of `ok` holdings (null when none is `ok`); `baseCents = Σ (valueCents − dayCents − laterCents)` of `ok` holdings, where `laterCents = centsOf(laterUnits × P)` (after-session lots are outside the base); `dayRatio = dayCents ÷ baseCents` (null when `baseCents ≤ 0`).
- `up`, `down`, `flat`: `ok` holdings by the sign of `dayCents`; `noChange`: held holdings that are not `ok`; `holdings`: all held.
- **Every holding adds up to the total:** the total is never rounded separately.

### 2.4 Closed markets, weekends, holidays, pre-open (D147: latest session)
- **Latest session (D147):** each listed holding shows **its latest session's move** until its next session's first bar arrives. On a Saturday, an ASX ETF shows Friday's move; a US listing shows its last US session; crypto shows the move since 00:00 Melbourne time today (D142). On a public holiday the ASX holdings keep the previous session. Before 10:00 on a weekday the ASX holdings keep the previous session (Yahoo returns the previous session until the open; the scout saw this for a US listing in its pre-market). **Bullion (D153) is measured from 00:00 Melbourne whatever the futures do, like crypto:** on a weekday the move since midnight; on a **Saturday** the move from midnight to the futures' Friday close (about 07:00–09:00 Melbourne, by DST) and flat after it; on a **Sunday**, and on a **Monday until the Sunday-evening reopen** (about 08:00–10:00 Melbourne), **0, flat since midnight** (the base is the closing spot, §5.4a). It does **not** carry Friday's move into the weekend: D153 defines bullion's day by the clock, as D142 does for crypto, and a "latest move" would need a second base (an earlier midnight) and would make Saturday's few hours of trading the whole weekend's figure (M24, M25).
- Only a **fresh** price gets a day figure (rule 3; the one exception is a daily fund's NAV within `FUND_DAY_MAX_WEEKDAYS`), so a Friday ASX price on a Tuesday is `stale`, never "today's" move.
- **A fetch with no bars never replaces a stored session** (§5.1); a fetch for an older session date never replaces a newer one.

### 2.5 The portfolio line (`portfolioLine`)
- Inputs: the `ok` holdings; for each, its points (native, ascending), `fxNow` (`prices.fx_rate`, 1 for AUD), `oldUnits`, its new lots, B and its `dayCents`.
- **Window:** if any holding with a line has a point whose date **in `DayChangeInput.timeZone`** is `localDate`, `from` = the earliest such point, floored to 5 minutes, and `to` = `generatedAt`; the line's `sessionDate` is `localDate`. Otherwise (no point today: a weekend without crypto, a weekday before 10:00 without crypto) the window is taken from **the line whose last point is the latest** (ties: the earliest first point): `from` = its first point, `to` = its last point, `sessionDate` = its `sessionDate`. Lines whose last point is earlier than `from` contribute their final value throughout (not 0).
- **Grid:** every 5 minutes from `from` to `to`, then `to` itself. At each grid time `t < to`, a line holding contributes `centsOf(oldUnits × (p(t) × fxNow − B) + Σ newLots remaining × (p(t) × fxNow − lot price))`, where `p(t)` is its last point at or before `t`; **before its first point it contributes 0** (unless the line ended before `from`, above). An `ok` holding without a line (a daily-priced fund) contributes its `dayCents` at every `t`. **At `t = to` every holding contributes its own `dayCents`**, so the last point is exactly `totals.dayCents` by construction (the chart's last point and the price P can differ: a separate CoinGecko call, or USD rounding of `native ÷ AUDUSD` against `native × (1 ÷ AUDUSD)`).
- `null` when no `ok` holding has a line. Downsampled to at most `LINE_MAX_POINTS` (§3.4) by `downsample` (§2.6).
- Between points a foreign holding is valued at today's FX: its currency move shows from the first point onward (documented approximation). **Bullion** takes part like crypto: its points are the stored AUD spot since 00:00 (each futures bar ÷ the `AUDUSD` bar at or before it, so the FX move is in every point), `fxNow` is 1, its units are ounces and its new rows are lots (§2.1). Its row always starts with `[midnight, B]`, so whenever a bullion holding is `ok` the window starts at 00:00 (as with crypto); on a day when the futures have not traded, its line is that one point and it contributes 0.

### 2.6 Lines per holding and downsampling
- A holding's `line` is given only when it is `ok`, its stored granularity is `5m` **and its kind is not `managed_fund`** (D141: a fund never has a line, even when its Yahoo symbol is exchange-quoted; such a fund also has `session.daily = true`; funds are fetched daily anyway, §5.1). **Bullion has a line** (D153): the AUD spot since 00:00 Melbourne against the dashed midnight base, like crypto's, refreshed by the 15-minute bullion scope (§5.6). `base` = its previous close in its own currency (crypto: the 00:00 price); `points` its stored points, **plus a final point `[priceAsOf, native price]`** when the price's as-of is later than the last stored point and in the same session (so a card's sparkline ends at the price shown).
- `downsample(points, max)`: with `n ≤ max` unchanged; otherwise the points at indices `round(i × (n − 1) ÷ (max − 1))` for `i = 0 … max − 1` (the first and last always kept, duplicates removed).

### 3.2 `market_quotes` (+2 columns)
`previous_close` text (null) and `previous_close_date` text (null): the FX series' own previous close and its session date (`AUDUSD` and `FX_<CCY>AUD` only; the bullion series leave both null (D153 does not use the settlement)). ### 3.3 The device store (not in the database)
`<DATA_DIR>/devices/devices.json` (folder 0700, file 0600; written by the app only), JSON `{ "version": 1, "devices": [ { "id", "label", "keyHash", "pairedAt", "lastUsedAt", "appVersion", "revokedAt" } ] }`. **Why outside `finance.db`:** a database restore swaps the whole file (Stage 7), so keys kept there would come back after their phone was removed, and they would travel to the NAS (Stage 8). `serverAddressKind`: `*.ts.net` or Tailscale's CGNAT range (100.64/10) → `tailscale`; a single-label name (MagicDNS short name) → `short_name`; `*.local`, `*.lan`, `*.home.arpa` or RFC 1918 → `lan`; `localhost` or 127/8 → `loopback`; else `other`. **Only `tailscale` keeps the key inside Tailscale:** a short name is also looked up by the Wi-Fi network's DNS when Tailscale is off (many home routers answer a DHCP host name with the LAN address), so `short_name` is treated like `lan` by the web (§8.2) and the app (§9.5). A full MagicDNS name (`<host>.<tailnet>.ts.net`) resolves only through Tailscale's resolver, so it fails closed when Tailscale is off.
**Test values for the rule table:** the CGNAT and RFC 1918 addresses are **built from octet arrays** in the schema test (an `ip(a, b, c, d)` join, with a comment; the Stage 8 R11 pattern), never written as dotted literals: the privacy guard flags every IPv4 literal outside loopback and RFC 5737. Host names: `umbrel`, `example.test`, `umbrel.example-tailnet.ts.net`.

### 4.2 Auth (the device key)
- The app sends **both** `Authorization: Bearer <key>` and `X-Joinr-Key: <key>` from 1.0.0, so a proxy that strips `Authorization` needs no app rebuild. The server uses `Authorization` when present, else `X-Joinr-Key`; when both are present and differ → 401 `DEVICE_KEY_INVALID` (counted as a bad key).
- Missing → 401 `DEVICE_KEY_MISSING`; not matching `MOBILE_KEY_RE`, or no device with `sha256(key)` → 401 `DEVICE_KEY_INVALID` (counts toward `BAD_KEY_RATE_MAX`; over it → 429 `MOBILE_RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After` for the rest of the window, for unknown keys only: a valid key is never limited); a removed device → 401 `DEVICE_KEY_REVOKED`.
- `X-Joinr-App-Version` (≤ 20 chars of `[0-9A-Za-z.+-]`) is stored as the device's `appVersion`.

## 5. Server spec — market data (server-market)

### 5.1 Yahoo (`providers/yahoo.ts`, `market/day.ts`)
- `yahooChartUrl(symbol)` → `…/v8/finance/chart/<sym>?range=1d&interval=5m` (every Yahoo quote: instruments, built-in series including the bullion futures, FX) **except daily requests and the three bullion inputs**: **`AUDUSD=X`, `SI=F` and `GC=F` use `range=2d&interval=5m`** (D153: the bars must reach back to 00:00 Melbourne at any hour; for these, `previousClose` is read from `meta.previousClose` first, which the probe showed is still the one-day previous close at 2d, so `AUDUSD`'s D143 previous close is unchanged; the price rules are unchanged; their `day` (for `AUDUSD`'s FX row) is built from the last trading period's bars only, and the full bar lists go to `bullionDayFrom`, §5.4a). Dividends and FX closes keep their own URLs.
- **Daily requests (managed funds; owner review O8):** the refresh marks each target of kind `managed_fund` with `daily: true` (a new optional field of the provider's quote request, `providers/types.ts`); the provider fetches those with **today's `range=5d&interval=1d` URL** (so a fund's web price path is unchanged) and attaches a `day` from `parseYahooDailyDay(result)`: the daily bars with a finite close > 0; the **last** one is the NAV (`sessionDate` = its date in `timeZone`, one point `[t, close]`), the **one before it** gives `previousClose` (null when there is none in the window: `no_base`); `granularity` `1d`; `regularStart`/`regularEnd` null; **`meta.chartPreviousClose` is never read for these** (at 5d it is the close before the window). Why: a fund whose NAV is a day late gets **0 bars** in a 1d/5m answer (the probe), so it would never have a day row and D141/D150 would show nothing. A daily request has no 1d/5m fallback (it is the old URL).
- `parseYahooChart` keeps its price rules unchanged and attaches `day?: QuoteDay` (new optional field on `Quote`) from `parseYahooDay(result)`:
  - `timeZone`: `meta.exchangeTimezoneName` when `Intl` accepts it, else `timeZoneFromSymbol`, else `UTC`.
  - `granularity`: `1d` when `meta.dataGranularity` is `1d` (or `1wk`/`1mo`), else `5m`.
  - Regular period: the last entry of `meta.tradingPeriods` (an array of arrays; flatten) → `regularStart`/`regularEnd`; **never** `currentTradingPeriod` (it points at the next session after a close).
  - `points`: `timestamp[i]` with `indicators.quote[0].close[i]` finite and > 0; for `5m`, only `regularStart ≤ t ≤ regularEnd` when the period is known (no pre/post bars); ascending; one per time; the last `DAY_POINTS_MAX` kept.
  - `sessionDate`: the last point's date in `timeZone`. **No points → no `day`.**
  - `previousClose`: `meta.chartPreviousClose`, else `meta.previousClose`, finite and > 0, else null. (`fulldayPrice`/`fulldayChange` are never read: they include US post-market.)
- **Fallback:** the provider asks once more with the old `range=5d&interval=1d` URL (the same spacing and cool-down; **counted as one attempt**) when the 1d/5m answer is any of: "No price in response" (a thinly traded listing with no bars and a degraded `meta`); **a `chart.error`** (Yahoo refuses 5-minute data for some funds and suspended listings; today `parseYahooChart` maps every `chart.error` to "Symbol not found", not retryable, so without this the price would stop updating and the instrument would drift into backoff, a regression for the web); **a non-2xx answer other than 429/403** (those start the cool-down as today). Only when the fallback also fails is the failure recorded (with the fallback's error). That quote carries no `day`.
- **The `0P…` probe (done at the owner review; shapes only in the private §6):** no AU `0P…` id could be found read-only (Yahoo's search and lookup do not list AU funds; four guessed ids answered 404 with `chart.error`, which the fallback above handles); a generic `0P…` fund on another exchange answered 200, `1d` granularity, **0 bars** at 1d/5m and dated NAVs at 5d/1d. Hence the daily requests above. The fallback stays for listings that refuse 5-minute data.

### 5.2 CoinGecko (`providers/coingecko.ts`)
- `fetchQuotes` unchanged (the batched `/simple/price`).
- New `fetchDayChart(id, signal): Promise<DayChartResult>` → `GET …/coins/<id>/market_chart?vs_currency=aud&days=1` (the provider's headers and timeout; 429/403 → rate-limited, as `fetchQuotes`).
- `cryptoDayFrom(prices, now, timeZone)` (pure, `market/day.ts`; **the zone is a parameter**, the dates come from `Intl`, never the process TZ): `midnight` = the instant of 00:00 on `now`'s date in `timeZone`; `base` = the last point with `t ≤ midnight` (null when none); `points` = `[midnight, base]` (when base is known) followed by every point with `t > midnight`, in unix seconds; `sessionDate` = `now`'s date in `timeZone`; `granularity` `5m`; `previousClose` = base. **An empty `prices` array, or one with neither a base nor any point after midnight → no row** (`null`). ### 5.3 The fake provider (`providers/fake.ts`; e2e and agents)
`fakePrice` unchanged (every existing test keeps its prices). Every fake Yahoo-style quote gets a deterministic `day`: zone `timeZoneFromSymbol(symbol) ?? 'Australia/Sydney'`; `previousClose = price × (1 − d)`, `d = ((fnv1a(symbol + ':prev') % 401) − 200) ÷ 10000`; for a **daily request** (a managed fund) `1d` with one point at 16:00 local of the latest weekday whose 16:00 is not after now (the NAV), `previousClose` as above; **for `SI=F`, `GC=F` and `AUDUSD=X`** (the 2d requests) 5-minute points over the last two weekdays to now, with no points from Friday 17:00 New York to Sunday 18:00 New York (the futures' weekend; `AUDUSD` from Friday 17:00 to Sunday 17:00 New York), so the midnight rules and the closed weekend run end to end in the fake; otherwise 5-minute points on today's session in that zone from 10:00 to `min(now, 16:10)` (the previous weekday's full session when before 10:00 or on a weekend), moving from the previous close to the price with a deterministic wiggle, the last point equal to the price. **The quote's `asOf` is the time of its last day point** (not `now`: on a weekend the fake's day is Friday's session, and §2.2 rule 4 ties the price to it). The futures' `asOf` follows the same rule (so the derived spot's as-of, the older of the futures' and `AUDUSD`'s, is the futures' last point; any existing suite that expects a fake series' `asOf` to be the clock's now is updated by server-market). FX series: `previousClose` equal to the price (no FX move). **`EXUS` is quoted in USD** (`fakeCurrency`; every other symbol as today, so no existing expectation changes), so the FX path (M4) runs end to end in the fake. `fetchDayChart` fake: the same rule from 00:00 local to now.

### 5.4 Writes (`refresh.ts`)
- `RefreshOptions.lite?: true`: only `instrumentIds`, or (the bullion scope) only `seriesIds` (`AUDUSD`, `SI_USD_OZ`, `GC_USD_OZ`) with their derived spot and `bullionDayFrom`; **no** other built-in series, cross FX, derived bullion, FX backfill or id search; conversion uses the stored FX (`point()`); cool-downs and counts as today; **backoff is read (an instrument in backoff is skipped) but never advanced: a lite failure writes nothing to `prices`** (the stored price and `last_status`, `last_error`, `consecutive_failures`, `last_attempt_at` are untouched) and is counted in the run's detail only. At a 5-minute cadence a transient fault would otherwise reach backoff in 15 minutes and block the hourly job too.
- **The price write never goes backwards (every job):** the price columns (`price`, `native_price`, `native_currency`, `fx_rate`, `as_of`, `fetched_at`, `source`) are kept when the stored `as_of` is **later** than the incoming one; the attempt columns are written as today. Runs commit at their end, so a slower hourly run would otherwise overwrite a fresher intraday price.
- **The newer-session rule (FROZEN):** in the existing IMMEDIATE transaction, for each `ok` instrument whose quote has a `day` (and which still exists with the captured kind and symbol): an incoming row for an **older** session than the stored one is ignored; a **newer** session replaces the row; the **same** session **merges**: `previous_close` = incoming ?? stored; `points` = the union by time (the incoming value wins on equal times), ascending, the last `DAY_POINTS_MAX`; the other columns from the incoming row. A row with neither a previous close nor any point is never written. FX series whose quote has a `day`: set `market_quotes.previous_close` and `previous_close_date`. **Bullion: the rows of §5.4a, with the same newer-session rule and the midnight-base merge below** (the hourly run and the bullion scope).
- **Midnight-based rows (crypto and bullion; FROZEN):** for the **same** session, **the first non-null base is kept** (`previous_close = stored ?? incoming`), never replaced by a later fetch; points merge as above. (Crypto's incoming base is the same midnight point anyway; for bullion this keeps the closing spot written while the futures were shut, so the figure does not jump at the reopen when bars-based and quote-based closes differ, M24.) Yahoo session rows keep `incoming ?? stored`.
- **§5.4a `bullionDayFrom` (pure, `market/day.ts`; the zone is a parameter, dates via `Intl`):** inputs: the futures' 2d bars, the `AUDUSD` 2d bars, the futures' quote (price, as-of), the derived spot (value, as-of), `now`, `timeZone`. `midnight` = 00:00 of `now`'s date in `timeZone`. **If the futures' quote as-of is at or before `midnight`** (no trade since midnight): AUD row base = the spot's value and points `[[midnight, base]]`; USD row base = the futures' price, the same one point. **Otherwise:** `F0` = the futures' last finite close at or before `midnight`, `X0` = `AUDUSD`'s last finite close at or before `midnight`, AUD base = `roundDerived(F0 ÷ X0)` (null when either is missing), points `[midnight, base]` then, for every futures bar after `midnight`, `roundDerived(F(t) ÷ X(last ≤ t))`; the USD row the same with `F0` and `F(t)`. `sessionDate` = `now`'s date in `timeZone`; a row with neither base nor points → none. Tested at 00:05 and 13:00 on weekdays, on Saturday after the close, Sunday, Monday before and after the reopen, **Sunday 04/10/2026 (its midnight is 14:00Z on 03/10, AEST) and Monday 05/10/2026 (13:00Z on 04/10, AEDT)**, the April change, and with no `AUDUSD` bar before midnight (base null; a later fetch fills it).
- `db/queries/dayQuotes.ts` (FROZEN): `loadDayQuotes(db): Map<number, DayQuoteRow>` (points parsed; a malformed JSON row → `points: []` and a warning with the instrument id only), **`loadSeriesDayQuotes(db): Map<string, DayQuoteRow>`** (the same, by series id: `XAU_AUD_OZ`, `GC_USD_OZ`, …) and `loadFxPreviousCloses(db): Map<string, { value: DecimalString; date: IsoDate }>`.

### 5.5 Crypto day writes
After a crypto scope's lite refresh, the service fetches each crypto target's day chart **sequentially** (`COIN_CHART_SPACING_MS` apart, stopping at a cool-down or the run's signal) and upserts the rows in one IMMEDIATE transaction at the end (the same newer-session rule and the midnight-base merge of §5.4: the first base of a session is kept; an empty or truncated series never clears a good row). The `/market_chart/range` endpoint is not used (unprobed; the merge rule covers the 25-hour day).

### 5.6 The `intraday` job (`market/intraday/{schedule,service}.ts`)
- Registered by the market service as `{ name: 'intraday', intervalMs: 0 }` in modes `live` and `fake` when `config.intradayRefresh`; its own timer calls `scheduler.run('intraday', 'schedule')` (which joins a run in flight). `MarketDataService.stop()` (new, FROZEN) clears the timer; server-api calls it first in `preClose`.
- **Slots:** every 5 minutes on the epoch's 5-minute marks (Melbourne offsets are whole hours, so these are local 5-minute marks in both AEST and AEDT) plus `INTRADAY_SLOT_OFFSET_MS`. The timer sleeps to the next slot (never more than `INTRADAY_WAKE_MAX_MS`); a slot that finds a run in flight is skipped. One start-up run after `INTRADAY_STARTUP_DELAY_MS` (crypto scope, plus the ASX scope when in its window).
- **Scopes, decided by the slot the timer targeted** (the 5-minute mark it slept towards, in the server's zone through `Intl`), **never by the wall clock at start** (a timer that fires late, after an event-loop stall, host sleep or an overrunning run, still runs its slot's scopes): **ASX** when the slot is Monday–Friday and `10:00 ≤ hh:mm ≤ 16:25` (public holidays are not known: the fetches then return the previous session, which the newer-session rule keeps); **crypto** when the slot's minute is a multiple of 15 (any day); **bullion** as in Targets below. A slot more than one step late is dropped (the next slot runs). Nothing due → no run is started. The repeated local hour 02:00–02:59 on 04/04/2027 runs its slots twice (harmless; tested).
- **Targets:** ASX: held, the effective source not manual, provider `yahoo`, `providerSymbol` with the `.AX` suffix, kind ≠ `managed_fund`. Crypto: held, not manual, provider `coingecko`, id resolved. **Bullion (D153; owner review O13):** when at least one bullion row is held, the slot's minute is a multiple of 15 and the slot lies in `INTRADAY_BULLION_WINDOW`: a lite run of the series `AUDUSD` and the futures of the metals in use (2d URLs), then the derived spot and `bullionDayFrom` for those metals. **Why 15 minutes, not hourly:** the hourly job would already give the right midnight base (the 2d bars reach back to midnight at any hour), but D153 puts bullion beside crypto, so its price and line should be as fresh as crypto's (15 minutes) and not up to an hour old on the card and widgets. **Yahoo calls:** (metals + 1) × 96 a weekday, at most **288** with both metals (≈ 0.75 s of spacing a run), ≈ 1,460 a week, on top of the hourly job's 3 an hour; none from Saturday 10:00 to Monday 06:00.
- **No two runs fetch the same ASX ids:** the ASX and bullion scopes are skipped while the `prices` job runs (counted `skipped`; both use Yahoo); and the `prices` job, before selecting its targets, **awaits an in-flight `intraday` run** (bounded by `INTRADAY_RUN_DEADLINE_MS`; the market service exposes the in-flight promise). Yahoo concurrency therefore never exceeds the provider's 2. The crypto scope may overlap `prices` (CoinGecko is batched); the never-backwards price write (§5.4) settles the order.
- Each run: `runRefresh(lite)` per scope, then the crypto day charts; deadline `INTRADAY_RUN_DEADLINE_MS`; status by the Stage 4 rules over its own counts; `detail = { asx: Counts | null, crypto: Counts | null, bullion: Counts | null, charts: { ok, failed, skipped }, dayRows: number }`.
- **Rate budget:** with N ASX holdings, N × 78 Yahoo calls a weekday (at 250 ms spacing, concurrency 2: about N × 0.15 s a run) on top of the hourly job; with M coins, 96 batched price calls and M × 96 chart calls a day; with bullion held, ≤ 288 Yahoo series calls a weekday (§5.6 Targets). A 429/403 starts the shared 15-minute cool-down (prices, dividends and intraday all pause).
- **Bounds:** `job_runs` keeps 500 rows per job (about 3 days of `intraday`); `day_quotes` is 1:1 and capped per row; nothing is held in memory between runs.

### 5.7 Configuration (`config.ts`)
`INTRADAY_REFRESH` (`true|false|1|0|yes|no`) → `Config.intradayRefresh`; default **true**, **false under `NODE_ENV=test`** (`TEST_DEFAULTS`); an explicit value is honoured under test. Off: no timer, no start-up run (a manual `scheduler.run('intraday')` still works). **This is the live kill switch:** if the 5-minute cadence draws Yahoo 429s, `INTRADAY_REFRESH: "false"` in the app service's environment in the store compose (a compose-only release) turns it off; a downgrade to 1.1.1 is refused after migration 0006 (`migrate.ts`: applied > known), so going back means restoring the pre-update backup. The RUNBOOK says this too.

## 6. Server spec — the phone API (server-api)

### 6.1 The device store (`mobile/devices.ts`)
- Loaded once at start-up into memory (the authority afterwards); every change (pair, revoke, the last-used flush) rewrites the file atomically: a temporary name in the same folder (mode 0600), `fsync`, rename, folder `fsync` where supported. The folder is created 0700 on first write.
- **Writes are serialised (FROZEN):** every store write is **fully synchronous** (`writeFileSync` + the existing `fsyncFile`, `renameSync`, `fsyncDir` helpers from `backups/copy.ts`), so no two writes interleave and each serialises the in-memory state at the moment it runs. No `await` sits between a decision and its write. (A promise queue would also do; the synchronous form is chosen because the file is < 10 KB and changes rarely.)
- **Unreadable or invalid file** at start-up: renamed to `devices.unreadable-<UTC stamp>.json` (never deleted), the store starts empty, `storeProblem: 'set_aside'` until the next successful write; one warning with the error `code` only.
- **A failed write, by change (FROZEN):**
  - **Pairing fails closed:** the device is not added, no key is returned, 503 `PHONE_STORE_FAILED`, `storeProblem: 'unwritable'`.
  - **A removal never fails:** the revoke is applied **in memory first** (the key gets 401 `DEVICE_KEY_REVOKED` at once), then persisted. If the write fails, the response is still 200 with `storeProblem: 'unwritable'` and `pendingRemovals` (the count of removals not yet in the file); the pending removal is retried on every later write, on the last-used flush and in `preClose`. A restart before it is saved would revive the phone, which the web callout says (§8.2).
  - A failed last-used flush changes nothing visible but `storeProblem`.
  - `storeProblem` returns to null after the next successful write.
- Lookup: a `Map` from `sha256(key)` (hex) to the device. Keys are 256-bit random values, so a fast hash is the right one (no password hashing); the key itself is never stored, logged or kept after the response.
- `lastUsedAt` is updated in memory on every authenticated call and flushed when it is `DEVICE_LAST_USED_FLUSH_MS` newer than the stored value, and in `preClose`.
- Removed devices keep their entry (`revokedAt`), newest `MOBILE_KEEP_REMOVED` only.

### 6.2 Pairing (`mobile/pairing.ts`)
- **Open** (`POST /api/phone/pairing`): 409 when `MOBILE_MAX_DEVICES` are active; else a new code (`crypto.randomInt` over the alphabet), `expiresAt = now + PAIRING_CODE_TTL_MS`, `failuresLeft = PAIRING_MAX_FAILURES`; any open code is replaced (`lastCancelled = { at, reason: 'replaced' }`). In memory only.
- **Exchange** (`POST /api/mobile/pair`, `bodyLimit: PAIR_BODY_LIMIT_BYTES`), in order (FROZEN): **no open code, or expired → 401 `PAIRING_CODE_INVALID` without counting** (there is nothing to guess, so junk posts cannot keep pairing blocked); the rate window, **counted only while a code is open** (over → 429 with `Retry-After`); the body (`code` string ≤ 40; `deviceName` ≤ 100; `appVersion` ≤ 20; unknown fields → 400) → `normalisePairingCode` (null → 401 `PAIRING_CODE_INVALID`, not 400, so a wrong code and a malformed one look the same); compare `sha256` digests with `timingSafeEqual`; a mismatch decrements `failuresLeft` (0 → the code is cancelled, `lastCancelled = { at, reason: 'failures' }`) → 401; the device limit → 409 (the code stays open); **then, synchronously and before any `await`, the code is marked consumed** (a second request with the same code now gets 401); generate the key and id, write the store (synchronous, §6.1; failure → 503 and the code is re-opened only if still inside its TTL); 201.
- **Label:** `deviceName` with Unicode **Cc, Cf, Zl and Zp** characters removed (control, bidi and zero-width format characters, line and paragraph separators), whitespace collapsed, trimmed, cut to `DEVICE_LABEL_MAX`; empty → `Android phone`.
- **Cancel** (`DELETE /api/phone/pairing`), **revoke** (`POST /api/phone/devices/:id/revoke`: `DEVICE_ID_RE` else 400; unknown → 404; already removed → 200 unchanged).

### 6.3 The key check (`mobile/auth.ts`) and read-only
- `routes/mobile.ts` is one plugin under `/api` whose `onRequest` hook checks the key (§4.2) for every route in it except `POST /mobile/pair` and the catch-all; `request.mobileDevice` is set on success.
- The catch-all `route({ method: ['POST','PUT','PATCH','DELETE','OPTIONS'], url: '/mobile/*' })` → 405 `MOBILE_READ_ONLY`; the static `POST /mobile/pair` wins over it (find-my-way). Only `GET /mobile/today`, `GET /mobile/device` (and Fastify's automatic `HEAD`) read.
- `/api/phone*` live in their own plugin, behind the Umbrel login and the existing cross-site guard; they never accept a device key.
- **Deny-by-default across plugins:** Fastify hooks are encapsulated, so a route under `/api/mobile` registered from any other plugin would get no key check while the proxy whitelist exposes it. `app.ts` adds a root **`onRoute` hook that throws at start-up** when a route whose path, lower-cased and percent-decoded, starts with `/api/mobile` is not one the mobile plugin declared (the plugin records its own routes in a set the hook checks).

### 6.4 Limits (`mobile/limits.ts`)
A small sliding-window counter (in memory; the clock injected): one for pair attempts, one for unknown keys. Restarting the server resets them (accepted: a restart also cancels the code).

### 6.5 The today builder (`mobile/today.ts`)
1. `createFinanceContext` (the same prices, engine and rows as the web pages), then `compute(kind)` for the four kinds: holdings and lots.
2. `loadDayQuotes`, `loadSeriesDayQuotes`, `loadFxPreviousCloses`; `fxPrev` per non-AUD row through `convertToAud('1', currency, prevRates)` (null when a rate is missing).
2a. **Bullion (`mobile/bullion.ts`, pure; D148):** from the same context, `otherAssetsInput().assets` (the rows' `pricing`, `ozPerUnit`, `purchaseDate`, `unitCost`, `currency`, `purchaseFxRate`) joined by id with `otherAssets().assets` (the engine results the Other Assets page shows); keep `pricing.source === 'bullion'` with `remainingUnits > 0`; group by `pricing.metal`; per metal a `BullionInput`: `spot` from `ctx.series` (`BULLION_HOLDINGS[metal].spotSeries`: value and as-of; `nativePerOz` from the futures series' value; `fxNow` = `convertToAud('1', 'USD', rates).fxRate` with the live `AUDUSD`); `day` from `loadSeriesDayQuotes` (`spotSeries`, `fxPrev` 1) and `nativeDay` (`futuresSeries`). It never reads a page response and never changes `assets/**`.
3. `computeDayChange` (§2) with `timeZone` (the server's IANA zone, `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`, the same value the response's `timeZone` carries), `localDate`, `generatedAt` and `fetched` (from `PriceItem.fetched` for every fetched, non-manual price: native currency and price, `fx_rate`, `as_of`).
4. Attach `key`, `code`, `symbol`, `name`, `items` (bullion: from `BULLION_HOLDINGS`), `position` (engine `costCents`, `unrealisedCents`, `unrealisedCents ÷ costCents` when cost > 0, `averagePrice`; bullion: the rows' sums, average per ounce), `session`, `line` (`downsample` to `LINE_MAX_POINTS`), `market` (§6.6), `freshness` (§4.3).
5. **The `features.*` page switches are ignored** (D95: preferences that hide pages, not data).

### 6.6 Market state (`mobile/market.ts`, pure)
On a weekday (server-local, the zone passed in): `07:00 ≤ t < 10:00` → `pre_open`; `10:00 ≤ t < 16:12` → `open`, **except** after `ASX_HOLIDAY_GRACE` when **held** ASX instruments have day rows and none has today's session date → `closed` (a holiday); **while a Yahoo cool-down is in force the state stays `open`** (missing bars are then not evidence of a holiday); otherwise `closed`. Weekends → `closed`. Sydney and Melbourne share the DST rules; a test asserts both zones give the same local times on every DST change date 2026–2030.

### 9.3 Network (`net`)
- OkHttp: connect 10 s, read 20 s; **`followRedirects(false)`**; headers `Accept: application/json`, `Authorization: Bearer <key>`, `X-Joinr-App-Version`, `User-Agent: JoinrFinance-Android/<versionName>`. The base URL is the paired origin; only the three §4.1 mobile paths are ever called.
- kotlinx.serialization with `ignoreUnknownKeys = true`; decimals as `String` → `java.math.BigDecimal` (never `Double`); points via a small serializer to `List<Pair<Long, String>>`.

### 9.4 Storage (`store`)

- `PairingStore` (DataStore): the encrypted key and origin, device id, label, paired-at. `TodayCache`: the last `MobileTodayResponse` JSON plus the phone's fetch time, encrypted, in app-private storage. `Prefs`: the sort, the Today tab (CARDS/LIST/MOVERS), each widget's chosen holding **by its `key`** (§4.3; never by `instrumentId`, which bullion lacks).

- **Revoked** (401 `DEVICE_KEY_REVOKED`, from the app or the worker): the same clearing, then the revoked state.

### 9.5 Pairing (`ui/pair`, `model/PairingUrl`)

### 9.6 The app lock (`ui/lock`, D139)

**Fail-closed rules (FROZEN):**
- **Only `isDeviceSecure == false` opens without a prompt.** Every other `canAuthenticate` result and every prompt error or cancel (`ERROR_USER_CANCELED`, `ERROR_NEGATIVE_BUTTON`, back, `ERROR_LOCKOUT`, `ERROR_LOCKOUT_PERMANENT`, `ERROR_HW_UNAVAILABLE`, …) keeps the **locked brand screen** with an **Unlock** button (≥ 48 dp) that shows the prompt again; content is never drawn and the activity never `finish()`es itself. ## 14. Risks & fallbacks
| Risk | Status / fallback |
|---|---|
| **app_proxy normalises a `..` path after the whitelist check** and forwards it, opening another route without the login | The app is safe on raw paths (verified over a socket; the corpus test). The proxy's behaviour is proved live before pairing (§11 step 9); any data → a compose-only 1.2.1 without the whitelist (the phone stops; nothing else changes) and the design is revisited. |
| **app_proxy strips `Authorization`** | The app sends both `Authorization` and `X-Joinr-Key` from 1.0.0 (§4.2); no rebuild. |
| **`/api/mobile/` is reachable from the whole LAN and tailnet without the Umbrel login** | A key is required for data; pairing needs a code the owner opens (5 minutes); everything is read-only; a deny-by-default route check (§6.3) stops a future route from slipping in under the whitelist. |
| **A key leaks** (a lost phone, a backup) | Keys are removable in Settings → Phone; stored only hashed on the server, encrypted on the phone, excluded from Android backup and from umbrelOS Backups; read-only by construction. |
| **Brute force on `/api/mobile/pair`** (reachable without the login) | A code exists only for 5 minutes after the owner opens it; 50 bits; 5 failures cancel it; 20 attempts per 10 minutes overall. Unknown keys are 256-bit and rate-limited. |
| **Cleartext HTTP** (the key in every request) | Safe only while the request travels inside Tailscale (WireGuard). **The pairing address is the full MagicDNS name (`*.ts.net`)**, which resolves only through Tailscale and so fails closed when Tailscale is off; a short name (`umbrel`) or a LAN address can be answered by the home router's DNS, and the 30-minute worker would then send the key over Wi-Fi in clear. The web shows an important callout for `short_name`, `lan` and `loopback`; the app warns before pairing and in Settings for any origin that is not `*.ts.net` or 100.64/10. HTTPS stays out of scope (as before). |
| **The device store cannot be written** (disk full, a permission problem after a restore) | Pairing fails closed (503); a removal always applies in memory at once and is retried until saved (§6.1); the web says so. |
| **Yahoo refuses 5-minute data** for suspended or unusual listings | The 5d/1d fallback runs on `chart.error` and non-2xx (not 429/403) before a failure is recorded (§5.1). Managed funds never use the 5-minute URL (daily requests, O8). |
| **Another app claims `joinrfinance://`** | The documented path is the in-app scanner (Google's, inside the app); the web never renders the pairing URL as a link; the RUNBOOK warns against camera-app scanning; "Paired: <label>" and the paired-phones list show an unexpected pairing. |
| **Yahoo rate limits** at 5-minute cadence | ≈ N × 78 extra calls a weekday at the existing spacing; a 429 starts the shared cool-down and the figures go stale visibly; the cadence is a constant (a later release can slow it). |
| **Yahoo returns no bars for a thinly traded listing** | The 5d/1d fallback keeps its price; its day figure is `no_base`. |
| **Yahoo's pre-open answer is not the previous session** (unverified before 10:00) | The no-bars and newer-session rules keep the stored session; worst case the day figure is `no_base` until the first bar. |
| **CoinGecko throttles per-coin charts** | Spaced 2 s, sequential, cool-down shared; a failed chart keeps the stored row; after midnight the coin is `no_base` until a chart succeeds. |
| **Android Doze delays the 30-minute widget work** | The widgets' age line is absolute ("updated Thu 14:10"), so it stays true while frozen; after 24 hours without a fetch the figures are replaced by "Open Joinr Finance to update"; opening the app refreshes. The RUNBOOK covers battery optimisation. |
| **The scanner's UI module is missing on a sideloaded APK** | `ModuleInstall` at the pair screen, failure and cancel handled (§9.5); by-hand pairing always works. |
| **A widget parcel too large** ("Can't load widget") | Density capped at 2×, `RGB_565`, ≤ 3 responsive sizes, a 400 KB budget test (§9.9). |
| **A lost signing keystore** | The next APK cannot update the installed one: uninstall, install, pair again. The RUNBOOK insists on a backup. |

| **Yahoo's futures roll to a new contract month** (`GC=F`/`SI=F` are continuous front-month series) | When the front month rolls during a Melbourne day, the bars after the roll are the new contract's, so gold's move since midnight can include the contract spread. Accepted (the web's spot moves the same way); rare (a few days a year). |
| **A live 2d answer on a Monday morning may lack the Sunday-evening reopen bars** (unverified; the Verified list) | The price comes from `meta`, so the day figure is right; the line then has only the midnight point and the final price point until the bars arrive (by Monday 04:00Z at the latest). A read-only probe on Monday 05/10/2026 settles it (private §6). |
| **No `AUDUSD` bar at or before midnight in the answer** (a feed gap) | The base is null for that fetch; a later fetch fills it (first non-null base kept); until then bullion is `no_base`. |
| **The bullion scope adds Yahoo calls** (≤ 288 a weekday) | Spaced and capped like every Yahoo call; a 429 starts the shared cool-down (bullion then falls back to the hourly job); `INTRADAY_REFRESH` turns the whole job off (§5.7). |
| **A fund with only one NAV in five days** (a holiday week, a weekly-priced fund) | No previous close in the 5d window → `no_base` (value counts). A longer range is a later option. |
| **Windows Gradle lint-cache locks** | `checkReleaseBuilds = false`; `gradlew --stop` after a release and before a clean (`android.mjs`). |
| **The phone has no Google Play services** (the scanner) | **Resolved:** the owner's phone has them (owner question 3, private §3). Contingency only (e.g. a replacement phone without them): pairing by hand always works; a ZXing switch would be an android-only change (`com.journeyapps:zxing-android-embedded` 4.3.0 in the version catalog in place of the code scanner, the `CAMERA` permission added to the manifest and the R8 rules, `ScanContract` in `ui/pair` in place of `GmsBarcodeScanning` and `ModuleInstall`, the scanner tests swapped, §15 gains "the app asks for the camera"); `minSdk` 30 is unaffected (an Android 10 phone would mean minSdk 29 and `BIOMETRIC_WEAK or DEVICE_CREDENTIAL` handled as two prompts). |

## 15. Behaviour changes in Stage 9 (owner can veto)
Numbering is stable. No template fixes this stage.
1. **Every Yahoo price fetch except a managed fund's is a one-day, five-minute chart** (with a five-day fallback): the same price, plus the previous close and today's bars. Managed funds keep today's five-day daily chart (their web price path is unchanged) and take their day from its last two NAVs.
2. **During ASX hours, ASX-listed holdings refresh every 5 minutes, and crypto every 15 minutes around the clock** — the web pages see these fresher prices too (one price everywhere, so the phone and the web always agree).
3. **The day figure is each holding's latest session** (D147).
4. **Units bought in the session count from their purchase price**, not from the previous close; sold units count nothing.
5. **Hand-priced, stale and unpriced holdings have no day figure** (their value and weight still count; "noChange" in the counts).
6. **Device keys live in `DATA_DIR/devices/`, not in the database**: a restore keeps phones paired and removed phones removed; a rebuilt Umbrel needs pairing again; umbrelOS Backups skip the folder.
7. **Removed phones stay listed** (the newest 20) with the date removed.
8. **The app locks again after 5 minutes in the background**; a phone without a screen lock opens the app with a warning.
9. **Release builds block screenshots and blank the app in recents** (`FLAG_SECURE`).
10. **Unpairing on the phone does not remove it on the server** (the phone cannot write); the app says to remove it in Settings → Phone.
11. **Day percentages show two decimals** on the phone (STYLE_GUIDE §8 says one; day moves are often under 0.1 %).
12. **Times on the phone are in the server's zone.**
13. **The QR code sits on a white tile** (scanners need dark on light): the one white surface in the web app.
14. **The pairing address is prefilled from the browser's address** and warns (an important callout) for a short name, a LAN or a loopback address: only the full Tailscale name (`*.ts.net`) is recommended. The app warns the same way before pairing.
15. **An `intraday` job** appears in the run history (about 150 runs a weekday, the newest 500 kept).
16. **The page-visibility switches (`features.*`) do not hide classes in the phone app.**
17. **The store manifest's Access paragraph changes** and the compose gains the one whitelist line.
18. **Version 1.2.0 with migration 0006**: the update takes the automatic pre-update backup.
19. **No wordmark in the widgets** (too small for the 24 px minimum); the app header uses the real wordmark. **Widget labels are uppercase without letter spacing**, in the system sans-serif, and widget figures in the system monospace (Glance cannot track letters or bundle a font).
20. **A `joinrfinance://pair` link opens the app** (with a confirmation, and a warning when it would replace the current pairing); the owner pairs with the app's own Scan button, never the camera app.
21. **Daily-priced funds show their latest NAV move** while the NAV is at most 2 weekdays old, even when the web marks the price stale (D150).
22. **Widgets replace their figures with "Open Joinr Finance to update" after 24 hours without a fetch**, show an absolute "updated <ddd HH:mm>" time, and are home-screen only (never the lock screen).
23. **A foreign holding's day figure uses the FX series' own previous close**, not the rate at the listing's previous close (an accepted approximation; §2.1).
24. **A fetched price never goes backwards:** a fetch whose as-of is older than the stored one keeps the stored price (every job; the web sees it too).
25. **Five-minute (intraday) failures do not count toward an instrument's backoff**; only the hourly job's failures do.
26. **Units bought after the stored session add nothing to the day figure**; a US purchase, dated by its Melbourne trade date, counts from the next NY session (§2.1).
27. **Removing a phone always takes effect at once**, even when the server cannot save the list; the web then says the removal is not saved yet.
28. **The five-day fallback also runs when Yahoo refuses the five-minute request** (for every price on the web too).
29. **Bullion is in the phone app** (D148) as one holding per metal (`GOLD`, `SILVER`), summing the Other Assets rows priced from the spot; it counts in the totals, weights, movers, the portfolio line and the widgets. Hand-priced other assets never appear.
30. **Bullion's day runs from 00:00 Melbourne like crypto (D153):** its base is the AUD spot at midnight (the currency move included); on a Sunday, and on a Monday until the futures reopen, it shows 0 (flat since midnight) rather than Friday's move; on a Saturday, the move from midnight to the Friday-night close.
31. **A bullion purchase dated today counts from its cost** (the session is the Melbourne day, so this is exact).
32. **The bullion series (`AUDUSD`, the gold and silver futures) are fetched with a two-day five-minute chart and, while a bullion row is held, every 15 minutes from Monday 06:00 to Saturday 10:00** (≤ 288 extra Yahoo calls a weekday); the web's spot (Other Assets) updates as often; the phone's bullion line is in AUD per ounce.
33. **Crypto may show no day figure (`no_base`) for up to one 15-minute slot just after 00:00 Melbourne** (Fixer, SPEC-6): the 00:00 run writes today's chart row, but CoinGecko's `last_updated_at` (the price's as-of) can still be before midnight, so rule 4 (the as-of date equals the session date) waits for the 00:15 run. Accepted (at most 15 minutes); pinned by a today test (00:05, as-of 23:59:40, today's row → `no_base`).

When bullion's day starts.** D148 takes the move from the futures' previous settlement, which is how Yahoo and brokers show gold and silver, but Yahoo's futures day runs midnight to midnight **New York** time, so the GOLD and SILVER figures start over at about 14:00–16:00 Melbourne time each weekday (and over the weekend show Friday's New York move until the Monday-morning reopen). (a) **Keep D148** (the settlement; the mid-afternoon restart); or (b) measure bullion **from 00:00 Melbourne** like crypto (D142), from the stored bars (a base taken from the last bar at or before midnight; more server work, and it no longer matches the settlement-based change other apps show). *Default: (a).* The coordinator may confirm at the demo; nothing in the build waits for it. **Answered (b) (D153):** bullion is measured from 00:00 Melbourne like crypto (applied: owner review O11–O16).
